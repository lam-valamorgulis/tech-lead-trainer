# Weekend 4: the Gym

Time: 45 minutes. Do it in the app.

> **Privacy first.** The Gym uses only the app's own scenarios. You may add notes from work in "Discuss in Claude". Then remove client names, code and internal numbers first.

## What to do

The Gym is in the app, so there is no file to fill in this weekend.

| Minutes | Step |
|---|---|
| 5 | Say each confusable pair out loud in one sentence (see below). |
| 30 | Open the Gym of town 1. Fight 5 battles in a row. Win 4 of 5 to get the badge. |
| 8 | Discuss your weakest battle in Claude (see below). |
| 2 | Write 2 sentences from memory about what you learned. |

If you lose the Gym, town 2 still opens. You can try the Gym again any time.

The Gym mixes concepts that are easy to confuse. Before you start, say each pair out loud in one sentence.
The same-origin policy is a browser rule: a page may only read responses from its own origin. Example: "The same-origin policy is the browser's rule. With CORS, the server opts in to share a response."
Source for this pair: [MDN: CORS](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTTP/Guides/CORS).

## After the Gym

Pick the battle you liked least. Tap "Discuss in Claude" for it.
Is the button not there yet? Then open a new chat at https://claude.ai/new. Paste the coach prompt below, and the battle in your own words.

## Coach prompt

```text
You are my tech lead coach.
Below is one Gym battle from my learning app: the scenario, the moves I picked, and why.
Do not show me the best answer yet.
Ask me ONE question at a time about my choices.
Wait for my reply.
Give hints, not solutions.
After three rounds, compare my thinking with the best move.
Then ask me to explain the trade-off again in my own words.
Show me two English mistakes and ask me to fix them.
```
