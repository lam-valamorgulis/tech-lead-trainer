# Tech Lead Trainer

A creature-catching learning game. A learner studies one town a month for 12 months to grow into a tech lead.

## Language

### Learning

**Lesson**:
The short teaching a learner sees before the first question about a new concept. It cites one trusted primary source and has a picture or diagram when the idea needs one.
_Avoid_: card, theory, background

**Look-back**:
The Lesson screen that holds the answer, shown again after a wrong first answer to a catch question. The learner then gets one more try.
_Avoid_: hint, review, card

**Town Intro**:
The big-picture lesson at the start of a town, with one diagram that shows how the town's concepts fit together.
_Avoid_: overview, chapter intro

**System Map**:
One diagram of a whole real web system, where each creature the learner has caught appears at its place.
_Avoid_: architecture map, world map

**Decision Card**:
A review item with a short situation and two options, where the learner picks one and a Reason.
_Avoid_: quiz card, trade-off card

**Skill**:
A tech lead ability the learner grows by using knowledge, such as debugging across systems or talking with a client. A town pairs its tech topic with one or two Skills.
_Avoid_: ability, soft skill, creature

**Trainer Card**:
The learner's own card, showing a rate for each Skill.
_Avoid_: profile, stats page

### Play

**Agent Diff Battle**:
A Battle where the opponent is a short piece of code written by an AI agent, with a hidden problem, and each move is a review verdict.
_Avoid_: code review battle, AI battle

**Constraint**:
The line in a Battle turn that states the limits of the situation, such as budget, team size, deadline or data, and so decides which move is best.
_Avoid_: context, condition, hoàn cảnh

**Battle**:
The daily fight against one opponent: a real situation where the learner picks moves turn by turn until the opponent's HP reaches 0.
_Avoid_: boss fight, match, fight

**Meeting Battle**:
A Battle where the opponent is a question or a push from a client or a senior manager, and each move is a possible answer. The Constraint is the situation of the meeting.
_Avoid_: client battle, talk battle

**Reason**:
One of three short explanations the learner picks after a move in a Battle, to say why the move works. A wrong Reason makes the hit weaker.
_Avoid_: why, explanation

**Faint**:
What happens to the learner's creature when its HP reaches 0 in a Battle; the next creature in the team takes its place.
_Avoid_: die, knocked out

**Boss**:
The big weekend task in week 1 of a town: the learner writes a full design doc for a large situation.
_Avoid_: boss fight, battle

### Improving the app

**Feedback**:
A free-text note a learner sends about the app itself, from any screen.
_Avoid_: report, review

**Report wrong**:
A note a learner sends about one piece of content that seems wrong.
_Avoid_: feedback, bug report
